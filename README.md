## Description

Located in the historic heart of Genova among the ancient *carruggi* (narrow alleyways), Trattoria delle Grazie is a cosy restaurant set inside the atrium of a restored patrician palace. The renovation was designed to bring out the building's original charm. The space doubles as a wine bar and is described as ideal for both a romantic dinner and a lively evening at the large communal bar counter. Reservations are recommended — the restaurant is consistently packed.
